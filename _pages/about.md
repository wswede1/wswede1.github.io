I'm a computational linguist and educator. I'm primarily interested in how technology can be used for educational purposes, particularly in language learning. I'm interested in all things language and edtech.

I hold an M.S. in Human Language Technology (HLT) from the University of Arizona. After earning my BA in Spanish from the University of Pittsburgh (H2P!), I spent several years teaching both Spanish and English. I've worked in public schools in Southern Chile, Spain, and middle and high schools in Northern Virginia. I also served as a graduate teaching assistant at both George Mason University and the University of Arizona.

After six years in the classroom, I decided to pivot toward technology and accepted a position in Data Curation at a small startup. My responsibilities began with data annotation for traditional machine learning models, and I eventually expanded into designing tasks and tools for the company's enterprise-grade flagship AI agent. This role immersed me in the rapidly evolving world of large language models, where I performed quality assurance on model outputs and served as the human-in-the-loop for model refinement. This hands-on experience with cutting-edge NLP systems and observing their capabilities and limitations firsthand sparked my interest in formally studying Human Language Technology.

While finishing the HLT program, I returned to the classroom to teach Spanish at a local middle school. This position allows me to share my enthusiasm for language while also educating the next generation (and co-workers) about responsible AI usage. My technical training in HLT at the UofA and my experience working directly with these systems in industry have given me a deep appreciation for how people interact with these technologies.

## What I work on

- **AI and NLP projects:** retrieval-augmented generation, language technology, speech synthesis, and machine learning.
- **Educational technology:** classroom applications and instructional tools, including games for Spanish instruction.
- **Resources for teachers:** reusable classroom tools, templates, prompts, guides, and AI agent skills.
- **Professional development:** workshops and micro-PD sessions on applied AI in education.

## HLT experience

During the MS HLT program, I gained practical experience through coursework and a professional internship. For my internship, I worked on a RAG system for Epistemix, a tech startup based in Pittsburgh, PA. Epistemix is a simulation and predictive analytics company that builds agent-based models and synthetic populations to help organizations make confident decisions in uncertain scenarios. Their work informs strategic planning in areas like precision public health, consumer behavior, and resource allocation by modeling complex population dynamics. The intention for the RAG system was to allow users to search and answer questions about the company, further promote their mission, and evangelize their methods and insights. The data used in this project came from the company podcast, "The Flux." ([Read the case study](/projects/internship/))

Another project I particularly enjoyed during the HLT program was a yoga pose recommendation system that allows users to find appropriate poses based on targeted body areas. ([Read the case study](/projects/ling508/))
